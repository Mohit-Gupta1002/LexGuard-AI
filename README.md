LexGuard AI is a legal-tech startup idea founded by Mohit Gupta, a Computer Science Engineering student at Presidency University. The platform serves as a startup’s first "Legal Co-Founder," tackling the "Contract Blindness" that leaves millions of Indian entrepreneurs vulnerable.  The project leverages a specialized AI engine to scan complex contracts and identify predatory clauses—such as hidden Intellectual Property transfers—in under 30 seconds. Built specifically for the Indian context, LexGuard AI offers plain-language explanations in 22 Indian languages.  By focusing on the 63 million MSMEs and 15 million freelancers who form the backbone of India’s economy, LexGuard AI ensures that legal intelligence is accessible to the 99% currently priced out by traditional firms. Our mission is to ensure no great idea fails simply because of a contract the founder couldn't understand
